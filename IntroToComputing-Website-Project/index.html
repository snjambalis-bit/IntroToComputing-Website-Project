What is Zenless Zone Zero?

Zenless Zone Zero is an urban fantasy action RPG developed by HoYoverse. Players take on the role of a Proxy, guiding agents through dangerous alternate dimensions known as Hollows. With fast-paced combat, stylish visuals, and a unique urban aesthetic, ZZZ offers an unforgettable experience.
